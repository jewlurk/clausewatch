## 1. What the Service does

The Service monitors publicly available regulatory instruments published by the Monetary
Authority of Singapore ("**MAS**"), detects textual differences between successive
published versions, and reports those differences at clause level. Where you have
recorded a mapping between your own internal control references and specific clauses, the
Service notifies you when a mapped clause changes.
